PocketKnives · logo concepts

Round seven: the same idea, a professional's kit caught being opened with the right knife picked out, redrawn as real everyday gear instead of a toy. Black Cordura shells with a high-visibility lining, ribbed webbing and elastic, double stitching, bar-tacks, rivets, snaps, grommets and zip teeth. The knives have hairline outlines, grind lines, stonewash and G10 texture. The selected knife is lifted, slightly larger and rim-lit instead of glowing, and the XP arrow is smaller. Tri-fold is replaced by a Hanging Kit. The concept-1…5.svg files are in Black & Orange; round six's red-and-blue kits are in git at 44a0769.

Rules kept from earlier rounds: no white or pale cross, no shield-shaped or plus-shaped kit, no pale bands crossing at right angles, no belt wrapped round a roll (that reads as WinRAR), and nothing like the installed PowerToys icon (no vertical colour stripes in a frame, no button row). Patches are blank: no text, no logos. Knife designs are original: no Spyderco-style round hole, no Emerson wave, no brand marks. Honest limit: at 16 px each mark is a kit outline, an accent band and one bright blade; a simplified tray glyph would follow once you choose. Weave textures are SVG patterns, checked in Edge only so far. Files: docs/logo-concepts/concept-1.svg … concept-5.svg (viewBox, no fonts, no raster, no external refs).
