PocketKnives · logo concepts

Round three: pocket knives lying face up on a cloth with a stripe, seen from directly above, with an XP cursor selecting one. The selected knife is lifted, glows and casts a deeper shadow, so it still reads as picked without the cursor. Same Windows XP Luna plastic as SnipSnap and QuickTooth. The concept-1…5.svg files are in Red & Blue; round two is in git at 4b5defc.

Red is your call, so the rules on red are: no red handles, no shield-shaped cloth, no white cross, and no stripe crossing another band at a right angle (the runner's knives lean, the roll's loops stop short of its stripe). The round mat has a scalloped edge so red and blue rings don't read as a roundel. Checked against the installed PowerToys icon (grey device, vertical colour stripes, three buttons): no stripes in a frame, no button row. Honest limit: at 16 px each mark is a cloth shape with a pale fleck for the selected knife; a simplified tray glyph would follow once you choose.
