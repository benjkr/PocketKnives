Red is your call, so the rules on red are: no red handles, no shield-shaped or plus-shaped cloth (no four-flap wrap opened out), no white cross, and no pale bands crossing at right angles; each kit's blue binding or elastic runs one way only. No belt wrapped round a roll (that reads as WinRAR); ties hang loose. Checked against the installed PowerToys icon (grey device, vertical colour stripes, three buttons): no stripes in a frame, no button row. Honest limit: at 16 px each mark is a cloth shape with a dark knife sliver and a pale blade streak; a simplified tray glyph would follow once you choose. Knife designs are original: no Spyderco-style round hole, no Emerson wave, no brand marks. The weave and stitching are tone-on-tone at thread scale, never pale bands crossing on red (no gingham or plaid). The texture uses SVG patterns, checked in Edge only so far.
