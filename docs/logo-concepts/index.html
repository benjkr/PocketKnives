PocketKnives · logo concepts

Round five: the same aggressive tactical folders as round four, now on real fabric instead of glossy plastic panels. Each cloth has a fine twill or canvas weave, hems and stitching, and soft shading where the knives press in. The tea towel has a hanging loop, the tool roll a bound edge and ties, the bandana a printed border, the mat padded scallops and a stitched ribbon, and the runner a frayed fringe. An XP cursor still selects the black-handled knife: larger, lifted and glowing. The concept-1…5.svg files are in Red & Blue; round four's plastic cloths are in git at 06197cb.

Red is your call, so the rules on red are: no red handles, no shield-shaped cloth, no white cross, and no stripe crossing another band at a right angle (the runner's knives lean, the roll's loops stop short of its stripe). The round mat has a scalloped edge so red and blue rings don't read as a roundel. Checked against the installed PowerToys icon (grey device, vertical colour stripes, three buttons): no stripes in a frame, no button row. Honest limit: at 16 px each mark is a cloth shape with a dark knife sliver and a pale blade streak; a simplified tray glyph would follow once you choose. Knife designs are original: no Spyderco-style round hole, no Emerson wave, no brand marks. The weave and stitching are tone-on-tone at thread scale, never pale bands crossing on red (no gingham or plaid). The texture uses SVG patterns, checked in Edge only so far.
