PocketKnives · logo concepts

Five ideas for the suite mark, drawn in the same Windows XP Luna plastic as the first two blades: glossy tube shading, Luna blue with Start-button green and amber, darker same-colour outlines and a soft down-right shadow. Each mark is a single SVG on a 64-unit grid. Every size below, including the 16 px tray and favicon mocks, is that same file.

1 · Fan-Outmy pickconcept-1.svg

Every blade swings off one rivet, fanned like a deck of cards. The dashed slot is where the next blade goes.

At 16 px: a blue handle with amber and green blades. The fan still reads; the dashed slot fades out.

3 · Pivot Kconcept-3.svg

The K of Knives: the stem is the handle and both arms are blades on one rivet. Trade-off: a third blade would break the letter, so this has the least room to grow.

At 16 px: a coloured K. Very legible, but it reads as a letter before it reads as a tool.
