# Logo concepts

How the Pulse logo was chosen (the blade was called Readout until then). Nothing in this folder
is used by the app or the README.

## The final logo

**The monitor.** A pixel icon in the suite's Windows XP style and the Graphite theme's colours,
with separately drawn 16, 32 and 48 px versions: an ECG trace on the LCD glass, its peak in the
hot colour, and under the glass the engraved name and the red-orange key. The 48 px version adds
an engraved heart and the key's `×`; the 16 px version has a groove in place of the name. The
files are in [`assets/`](../../assets):

- **`icon.svg`:** all three versions in one file. It shows the version that fits the size it's
  drawn at: the 16 px version below 32 px, the 32 px one below 48 px, and the 48 px one from
  48 px up.
- **`pulse.ico`:** for Windows. It holds 16, 32 and 48 px, 64 and 96 px (the 32 and 48 px
  versions doubled) and 256 px (the 48 px version at 5×).

It's pixel art. Scale it by whole numbers with nearest-neighbour, and don't redraw or recolour it.

## The rounds, oldest first

- **`round-1.html`:** five concepts (the screen, the heart, the calculator, the tile, the key)
  beside the navy calculator that was Readout's logo. The user asked for more of 1 and 4.
- **`round-2.html`:** three takes each on the screen and the tile. The user picked 1b, the
  monitor; the final logo is that one with a horizon glare on the glass and the name at 32 px.
